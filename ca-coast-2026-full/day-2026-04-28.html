Morro Rock materialized across the glassy bay as we set out on the Bluff Trail, watching kayakers thread between sea-otter-dotted waters before dropping down to rocky tide pools in the afternoon. We ended the day on the cliffs at sunset, the light turning the sandstone orange.

Highlights:

  • Morro Rock — a 581-foot volcanic plug and one of the "Nine Sisters" chain of ancient peaks; it serves as a protected peregrine falcon sanctuary
  • Bluff Trail, Montana de Oro — the park's name ("mountain of gold") comes from the golden wildflowers blanketing its clifftops each spring
  • Gumboot Chiton — the world's largest chiton, it lacks a visible shell externally and can live over 20 years
