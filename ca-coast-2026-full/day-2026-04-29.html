Early light brought two wild cottontails foraging on the pavement outside our lodging before we set out on bikes along the coast, stopping at a pier, sea stacks, and tide pools through a long, sunny afternoon.

Highlights:

  • Pismo Beach — one of California's last open beaches where vehicles can legally drive on the sand; also historically the center of the Pismo clam harvesting industry
  • Dinosaur Caves Park — named for a now-demolished 1930s roadside attraction featuring a concrete dinosaur that once doubled as a tourist gift shop above its sea caves
  • Black Oystercatcher — uses its laterally flattened bill to pry open mussels and limpets; its entire global population is estimated at fewer than 11,000 birds
