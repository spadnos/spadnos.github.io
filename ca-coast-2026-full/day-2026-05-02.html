Fog still clung to the rocks at noon as we picked our way along the coast before driving inland to Paso Robles for the evening. The day split cleanly between a raw shoreline scramble and a crowd-filled light festival at dusk.

Highlights:

  • Moonstone Beach — a narrow boardwalk beach named for the translucent moonstones once found tumbled in its surf; the whimsical carved stump faces tucked into the plantings are a recurring local folk-art tradition
  • Sensorio Field of Light — Bruce Munro's site-specific installation uses over 58,000 solar-powered fiber-optic stems spread across 15 Paso Robles acres; a second phase added large geometric sculptural lanterns distinct from the original stem field
