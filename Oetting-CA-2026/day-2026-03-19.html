Soft light and mudflats set the tone as I walked the Baylands trails mid-morning, scanning grasses and shallows for shorebirds before heading home by late afternoon.

Highlights:

  • Palo Alto Baylands Nature Preserve — the largest intact marshland remaining in San Francisco Bay, managed partly through a historic 1960s landfill boundary that now doubles as a levee trail
  • San Francisco Bay tidal mudflats — exposed at low tide, these flats support some of the Pacific Flyway's densest shorebird concentrations, with over 100 species recorded seasonally at this stretch of the South Bay
