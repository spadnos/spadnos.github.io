Travelers

Rachel, Hazel, Greg, Sunil, Colleen, Steve

Morning fog still clung to the hillside as we moved between the guest cottages and main grounds of Hearst Castle, then dropped down to the coast to watch elephant seals on the beach below.

Highlights:

  • Casa del Sol — one of three Spanish Colonial Revival guest houses on the Hearst Castle estate, designed by Julia Morgan and named for its sun-facing position on the hill
  • Casa del Monte — the smallest of the three guest cottages, featuring hand-painted antique tiles sourced from Spain and Portugal
  • Piedras Blancas Elephant Seal Rookery — a year-round colony where males can weigh up to 5,000 lbs; the colony unexpectedly established itself here in 1990 after decades of absence from California
